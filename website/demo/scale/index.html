WHAT IS COUNTED

Captured message counts

Counts come from received records assigned to disjoint source ranges. Retries and duplicate keys do not increase the message count. Events are shown separately.

WHAT STAYS PRIVATE

Private source archives

Full message bodies, authors, source IDs, account identifiers and original archives remain on the private test host. Only reviewed short excerpts, aggregate measurements and signed checkpoints are shown here.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES

Signature verification

Signatures bind receipts to the supplied key. They do not certify message origin, completeness or legal admissibility. Collection uses text and metadata; attachment bytes are excluded.
